# Task 1.14 — Dashboard: partner area

**Phase:** 1 — Studio MVP · **Depends on:** 1.7, 1.10 · **Design refs:** TONG-QUAN.md §21 (roadmap item 8)

## Goal
Partners manage listings, calendar and revenue themselves.

## Scope
- [ ] Listing management (publish/hide own posts — except admin-hidden)
- [ ] **Master calendar**: every booking across all resources by day/week, filterable by listing type, quick calendar blocking
- [ ] Booking list + request-to-book approve/decline
- [ ] Revenue screen: balance, journal lines, payout history

## Definition of Done
- Partner sees a new booking on the master calendar immediately; blocking a slot removes it from public availability
